# Local Business Customer Analytics

## Project Overview

Local businesses often collect customer and sales data but rarely analyze customer purchasing behavior systematically. This makes it difficult to understand customer preferences, identify high-value customer segments, and design effective promotions.

The **Local Business Customer Analytics** project aims to analyze customer purchasing behavior and provide useful business insights through data analysis and visualization. The project will help local businesses understand sales patterns, customer segments, purchasing frequency, and customer value so that they can make more informed business decisions.

## Problem Statement

Local businesses rarely analyze customer purchasing behavior systematically, making it difficult to design effective promotions or identify high-value customer segments. Better analytics can help businesses understand their customers, improve promotional strategies, and support business growth.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze customer purchasing behavior.
* Identify high-value customer segments.
* Understand purchasing patterns and trends.
* Analyze customer spending and purchase frequency.
* Identify products or categories that perform well.
* Provide meaningful insights through dashboards and visualizations.
* Support better promotional and business decisions.

## Capstone Day-by-Day Plan

### Day 1 — Project Setup

* Set up the GitHub repository.
* Create the project structure.
* Prepare the README.
* Define the project objectives and requirements.

### Day 2 — Dataset Collection

* Identify a suitable customer and sales dataset.
* Download or collect the dataset.
* Understand the available columns and data types.

### Day 3 — Data Understanding

* Explore the dataset.
* Check the number of records and columns.
* Understand customer, transaction, product, and sales information.

### Day 4 — Data Cleaning

* Handle missing values.
* Remove duplicate records.
* Correct inconsistent data.
* Convert columns into appropriate data types.

### Day 5 — Exploratory Data Analysis

* Analyze sales trends.
* Study customer purchasing frequency.
* Analyze customer spending behavior.
* Identify important patterns in the data.

### Day 6 — Customer Segmentation

* Group customers based on purchasing behavior.
* Analyze customer value.
* Identify high-value, regular, and low-engagement customer groups.

### Day 7 — Product and Sales Analysis

* Identify best-performing products or categories.
* Analyze sales contribution.
* Study purchasing patterns across different products or categories.

### Day 8 — Dashboard / Visualization

* Create charts and visualizations.
* Design a dashboard showing important business metrics.
* Present customer and sales insights clearly.

### Day 9 — Insights and Recommendations

* Summarize important findings.
* Identify opportunities for targeted promotions.
* Develop data-based recommendations for local businesses.

### Day 10 — Testing and Final Documentation

* Review the analysis and visualizations.
* Check the accuracy of the results.
* Improve the dashboard and documentation.
* Prepare the final project presentation and submission.

## Expected Outcome

The completed project will provide a customer analytics solution that helps local businesses understand purchasing behavior, identify valuable customer segments, recognize sales patterns, and make more informed promotional and business decisions.
